1 Overview & Full App Capabilities

Farm Manager (ফার্ম ম্যানেজার) is a comprehensive digital farm management, livestock analytics, and agricultural trading utility designed for poultry (Broiler, Sonali, Layer), dairy, beef cattle fattening, and fish farmers across Bangladesh and South Asia.

Key Integrated App Modules:

  • Flock Batch Management: Multi-batch tracking from day-old chick (DOC) to sale.
  • Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR): Automated benchmark calculation based on live bird weight and feed intake.
  • Mortality & Health Logs: Daily mortality tracking and biosecurity alerts.
  • Medicine & Vaccines: Routine vaccination schedules and withdrawal periods.
  • Daily Feed & Stock: Feed bags and kilograms consumption records.
  • Financial Accounting: Automated Profit & Loss, expense logs, and sales calculations.
  • Digital Cash Memo Generator: Instant printable customer receipts with shareable links.
  • Customer Dues Ledger: Borrower tracking and payment status.
  • Chick Market & Live Rates: Hatchery booking rates and dealer listings.
  • Farmer Hat / Marketplace: Direct farmer-to-buyer livestock trade.
  • Feed & Medicine Store Directory: Verified local agricultural shop listings.
  • Veterinary Doctor Network: Direct tele-consultation contact directory.
  • Voice Log Assist: Bengali voice input for frictionless on-farm record keeping.

2 Information We Collect & Process

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver offline-capable farm accounting and cloud synchronization:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, optional contact number, and unique Firebase User ID (UID).
  • Farm Operational Records: Flock sizes, daily feed weight, mortality numbers, egg collections, medicine schedules, expense categories, sales revenues, and customer debt ledgers.
  • Community Marketplace Submissions: User-uploaded livestock photographs, asked prices, farm location/district, and seller telephone numbers voluntarily submitted for public listing in the Farmer Hat or Chick Booking sections.
  • Temporary Voice Audio: Spoken audio processed in real-time for speech-to-text conversion. Raw audio is never stored permanently.

3 Device Permissions

🎙️ RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone)

Used strictly when tapping the microphone button to dictate farm expenses or daily notes. No audio is recorded in the background or shared with third parties.

🌐 INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required to securely synchronize your farm accounting documents with Google Cloud Firestore and download real-time market chick prices.

4 Google AdMob & Advertising Policy

To keep the Farm Manager platform completely free and accessible for smallholder farmers, we display advertisements served by Google AdMob.

  • Zero Accidental Click Discipline: Ads are strictly prohibited from overlapping with critical navigation controls, floating action buttons (FAB), or data save buttons. Banners are automatically hidden during data input and modals.
  • Post-Action Interstitials: Interstitial ads are only presented after legitimate user actions (such as completing an expense save or printing a memo) with appropriate cooldown intervals.
  • Google Families Policy: All advertisements comply with Google Play Developer content policies, ensuring safe, relevant agricultural and business ads.

5 Marketplace, Chick Booking & Community Rules

All users participating in the Chick Market, Store Directory, and Farmer Hat must strictly adhere to the following community guidelines:

🚨 Rule 1: NO ADVANCE PAYMENTS WITHOUT VERIFICATION

Never send advance money or booking deposits via bKash, Nagad, or bank transfer to unknown sellers or hatcheries before physically inspecting the birds/goods or verifying the seller in person. Farm Manager is an open directory and will not be liable for unauthorized financial transfers.

⚖️ Rule 2: Honest Pricing & Authentic Listings

Dealers and hatcheries must publish genuine, accurate market rates. Posting fake rates, bait prices, or spam listings will result in immediate deletion and account bans.

🐓 Rule 3: Biosecurity & Responsible Farming

Farmers are encouraged to observe strict biosecurity, adhere to veterinary withdrawal periods before selling medicated livestock, and prioritize animal health.

6 Data Security & Cloud Storage

We employ industry-standard cloud security practices:

  • End-to-End Encryption in Transit: All API communication uses TLS 1.3 encryption over HTTPS.
  • Google Firebase Security Rules: Role-based access ensures that each user has isolated, authenticated access to their private farm financials and flock logs.
  • Zero Plaintext Storage: User authentication credentials are secure via Google Firebase Auth.

7 User Rights & Account / Data Deletion Policy
