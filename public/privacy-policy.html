1 Overview & Scope

Digital Khamar Pro ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a smart farm management and livestock analytics application developed to assist poultry, dairy, cattle, and aquaculture farmers in tracking flock batches, feed consumption, mortality, medication, cash expenses, sales records, customer dues, and livestock marketing.

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal and agricultural information you provide while using our application. We only collect the minimum amount of data required to deliver core farm accounting, analytical calculations, and telemedicine/marketplace features.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us when setting up an account or utilizing farm management features:

A. Personal & Account Information

  • Name / Profile Name: Used to personalize your farm profile and cash memos.
  • Email Address: Used for secure Firebase Authentication, login verification, and administrative communications.
  • Phone Number: Used optionally for account recovery, cash memo seller headers, and marketplace/doctor contact listings.
  • Profile Photo (Optional): Your Google account avatar or custom avatar.

B. Farm Operational & Financial Records

  • Batch Records: Bird/livestock type, quantity, breed, start date, target harvest weight.
  • Daily Operational Logs: Daily feed intake (kg/bags), mortality counts, medicine & vaccine usage, body weight measurements.
  • Financial Data: Production costs, day-old chick purchases, feed expenses, medication bills, product sales receipts, cash memos, customer dues (বাকির খাতা).

C. Community Marketplace & Doctor Listings

  • Marketplace Ads: Product titles, livestock quantities, asking prices, division/district locations, seller contact phone/WhatsApp numbers, and user-provided item photos.
  • Doctor / Agro Store Directory: Practitioner degrees, specialty, clinic name, district, consultation hours, and contact details voluntarily published for farmers.

D. Technical & Device Information

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address, device model, operating system version, browser type, and timestamps generated automatically during standard network requests to Google Firebase servers.

3 How We Use Your Data

We process and use the collected data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Mathematical Farm Analytics: Calculating Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR), Mortality Percentage (%), Average Daily Gain (ADG), Feed per Bird, and Net Profit/Loss.
  • Account Sync & Multi-Device Access: Synchronizing your farm records securely across your smartphone, tablet, or web browser in real-time.
  • Offline Caching: Permitting continuous record-keeping even in rural areas without active internet connectivity.
  • Customer Support & Verification: Responding to user inquiries, verifying manual subscription payments (e.g. bKash/Nagad TrxID), and resolving technical issues.
  • No Data Selling: We NEVER sell, rent, or monetize your private farm financial records or contact info to third-party marketing companies.

4 Device Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions only when explicitly activated by you:

🎙️ RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone)

Used exclusively when you tap the Voice Logging button to speak Bangla or English farm notes. Audio is temporarily processed for speech-to-text transcription and is never permanently recorded or stored.

🌐 INTERNET & NETWORK_STATE

Required to securely transmit and synchronize your encrypted database documents with Google Firebase Cloud Firestore and download live market price updates.

5 Third-Party Services & Firebase

We leverage reputable, enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Provides encrypted cloud database storage (Cloud Firestore) and user authentication (Firebase Auth). Governed by Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Gemini API (Google Cloud): Used for intelligent natural language parsing of voice logs and optional veterinary advisory suggestions.
  • WhatsApp / Phone Intent: External links allowing direct peer-to-peer farmer-to-doctor or farmer-to-buyer communication via native device dialer or WhatsApp.

6 Data Security & Protection

We employ strict security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your device and our servers use HTTPS with industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Granular Database Access Rules: Firestore Security Rules enforce that each user can only read, write, or modify their own private batches, expenses, and sales documents.
  • No Plaintext Passwords: Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth, utilizing industry-standard credential hashing.

7 Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

You maintain full ownership of your data at all times:

  • Item-Level Deletion: You can delete any batch, feed log, medicine record, expense, or marketplace post directly within the App at any time.
  • Complete Account & Data Wipeout: If you wish to delete your entire account, profile, and all associated cloud database records, you can submit a deletion request by emailing sr0632890@gmail.com or contacting 01410991934 with your registered email address.
  • All personal data and farm records will be permanently purged from our active Firestore databases within seven (7) business days.

8 Children's Privacy

Digital Khamar Pro is an agricultural productivity tool intended for adult farm owners, poultry/livestock workers, and business operators. The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
